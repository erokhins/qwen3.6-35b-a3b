Why it is recorded: `tools/moe_stats.py --near-tie` measures the 8th/9th selection margin over
256 generated tokens, while the pinned rows are 32. `make_input` draws `torch.randn(T, H)` from a
private seeded generator and rescales each row's RMS, so the T=32 fixture is exactly the first 32
rows of the T=256 fixture. That is what makes the near-tie population a statement *about the pinned
rows* rather than about a different input; see `speccheck/results.md` §Near-tie and utilisation.
